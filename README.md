## What this is

MobinWiki is one person's curated body of thought and reference, kept online so it can outlive the person who made it. It has two parts, deliberately kept separate:

### 1. Insights
How I see the world: my own ideas, opinions, interpretations, and conclusions. These are personal. They are mine as written. They are not presented as neutral fact, and they should not be treated as such.

### 2. Facts
Compact, gathered reference notes — practical how-tos, facts collected on a topic, and distilled summaries meant to be useful and dense. These aim to be accurate and are date-stamped, because facts go stale.

## How it works — while I am alive

- I write and maintain everything in **Markdown**, edited offline in **Obsidian**. The offline copy is the source of truth.
- The site is published from that source using **Flowershow**.
- **Visitors cannot edit anything.** To suggest an edit, message me on Telegram at **@Bob_Belcher**. If I approve it, the change is published and credited to you or to the source you provide.

## How it works — after I am gone

I have arranged for this archive to be handed to a designated successor, who holds the account credentials and the offline copy. Their job, as I have asked them, is:

- Keep the site online as long as reasonably possible.
- If hosting ever lapses, preserve the Markdown source and deposit it somewhere durable (an archive, a library, a public repository). The text matters more than the website.

## Why I built this

I wanted a second brain — a place to store what I learn and think — and I did not want my ideas to die with me. This site is my attempt to keep them alive.
