# Facts

This section contains compact, gathered reference notes: practical how-tos, facts collected on a topic, and distilled summaries meant to be useful and dense.

## What belongs here

- How-to notes and practical procedures
- Facts gathered on a topic and compressed for quick reference
- Summaries of research, rules, deadlines, requirements, etc.
- Anything meant to be accurate and useful rather than personal

## What does not belong here

- Personal opinions or interpretations (those go in `Insights/`)
- Speculation presented as fact

## A note on accuracy and dates

Facts go stale. Every note in this section should carry a header with:

- **Start:** the date the note was created
- **Last Update:** the date it was last edited

These notes aim to be accurate, but they reflect what was known at the date stamped in their header.
