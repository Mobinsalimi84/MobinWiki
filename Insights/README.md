# Insights

This section contains Mobin's personal insights: how I see the world, my own ideas, opinions, interpretations, and conclusions.

## What belongs here

- Original ideas and arguments
- Personal interpretations of events, books, or fields
- Opinions and conclusions I have reached
- Frameworks and mental models I use

## What does not belong here

- Neutral, gathered facts (those go in `Facts/`)
- How-to notes or practical reference material
- Anything presented as objective fact rather than personal view

## A note to future readers

These are personal views, written by one person at a particular time. They are preserved as written. They are not neutral fact, and they should not be treated as such. If additions are ever made by others, they will be clearly labeled as not mine.
